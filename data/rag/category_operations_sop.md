## Clothing Operations

Clothing revenue is highly festival-sensitive. Merchandising should align promotional calendars with Diwali, Holi, and regional festivals such as Onam in Kerala.

## Furniture Operations

Furniture has lower order frequency but higher ticket size. Misses often trace to delivery-state concentration rather than nationwide demand collapse.

## Electronics Operations

Electronics line items frequently include multiple units per order. Investigate sub-category mix (Phones vs Printers) before concluding a category-wide miss.

## Investigation Checklist

When investigating a target miss:

1. Compare actual vs target by category for the month.
2. Drill down to the largest contributing state.
3. Review anomaly flags for the same window (association only).
4. Compare 30-day forecast total to target (not causal proof).
5. Check festival calendar overlap for the month.
