# Target Setting Policy (Synthetic)

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT** — Created for MarketLens AI demo purposes. Not a real company policy.

## Monthly Target Revision Process

Category targets are reviewed monthly by the commercial planning team. Revisions require finance approval when the change exceeds 10% of the prior month target.

## Electronics Category Guidance

Electronics targets were increased from ₹9,000 to ₹16,000 per month starting January 2019 to reflect expanded phone and printer assortment. Teams should expect higher scrutiny on miss rates after this step-change.

## Shortfall Escalation

When a category misses target by more than 10% in a month, the category manager must submit a variance explanation within 5 business days covering region mix, festival overlap, and inventory constraints.

## Forecast Alignment

Forecasts with horizon greater than 30 days are treated as directional only. Operational decisions should prioritize 7-day and 30-day horizons unless explicitly approved by leadership.
